

## **Introduction to Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision**



#### **Artificial Intelligence(AI)**



Artificial Intelligence is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.



##### **Computer Vision**



Computer Vision is a field of AI that enables computers to interpret and understand images and videos.
